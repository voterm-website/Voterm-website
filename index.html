WHO WE ARE

A Mission Founded on Christ.

Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission exists to proclaim Jesus Christ to every people, every nation, every generation and every language until He returns.

Our ministry is built around evangelism, discipleship, prayer, Christian education, missionary work, compassion and the raising of faithful voices for Christ.

01

Proclaim Christ

Preach the Gospel faithfully and without compromise.

02

Build Disciples

Help believers grow in Scripture, prayer, character and maturity.

03

Raise Voices

Discover, train, mentor and release faithful servants.

04

Serve Humanity

Demonstrate the Gospel through compassion and practical service.
