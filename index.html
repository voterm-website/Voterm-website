Who We Are

Voice on the Rock

Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission exists to lift up the name of Jesus Christ and proclaim the uncompromised Word of God.

We believe that the Church is called to preach the Gospel, disciple believers, strengthen families, serve communities and prepare people for the return of Christ.

Our desire is to be a faithful voice standing firmly upon the Rock — Jesus Christ.
