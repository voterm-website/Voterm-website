Foundational Pillars

Divine Purpose & Governing Principles

Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission (VOTREM) is governed by sacred scriptures and dedicated to stewardship under the absolute Lordship of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision Statement

"To establish, beyond religious traditions and human philosophies, a people whose lives, voices, and ministries faithfully proclaim the pure Word, purpose, and character of Jesus Christ, standing upon Him alone as the Eternal Rock."

ARTICLE I, SECTION 1.6

The Caretaker's Principle

"Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission belongs to Jesus Christ alone. No leader, minister, or trustee shall ever regard this Mission as personal property. Leadership is sacred stewardship, never personal ownership."

1 CORINTHIANS 4:2 • CARETAKER'S CHARGE

The Raising of Voices

"One of the principal assignments of VOTREM shall be to discover, encourage, equip, and release men and women whose voices shall faithfully proclaim Jesus Christ to all nations without compromise."

ARTICLE I, SECTION 1.10

Our Core Identity & Mission Mandate

We stand for the uncompromised Gospel, leading sinners to genuine repentance, restoring backsliders, discipling believers into spiritual maturity, demonstrating Christ's love through practical compassion, and preparing God's people for the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.
