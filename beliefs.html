Statement of Faith

What We Believe

Biblical convictions that guide the faith and ministry of VOTREM.

The Scriptures

We believe the Holy Bible is God’s authoritative Word and the foundation for faith and practice.

God

We believe in one eternal God, revealed as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Lord and Saviour, who died and rose again.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers for holy living and faithful service.

The Church

We believe believers are called into worship, fellowship, discipleship and mission.

The Great Commission

We believe the Gospel is to be proclaimed to all people and nations.

Holy Living

We believe believers should grow in obedience, love, integrity and Christlike character.

Christ’s Return

We believe Jesus Christ will return and God’s purposes will be fulfilled.
