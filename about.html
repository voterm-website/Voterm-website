Built to proclaim the voice of Christ.

Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission is a Christian evangelical ministry committed to biblical truth, discipleship, prayer and the Great Commission.

Who We Are

Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission is dedicated to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ and teaching the Holy Bible in a clear, faithful and practical way. Our desire is to see people encounter Christ, grow in spiritual maturity and live as faithful witnesses.

Our Vision

Standing Upon the Rock, Proclaiming His Voice.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, teach biblical truth, strengthen believers through prayer and sound teaching, develop Christian resources, and equip the Church for the Great Commission.

Our Values

  • Faithfulness to Scripture
  • Christ-centred living
  • Prayer and dependence on God
  • Holiness and integrity
  • Discipleship and spiritual growth
  • Evangelism and service
