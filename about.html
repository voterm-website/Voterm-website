About VOTREM

Who We Are

A Mission standing upon the Rock and proclaiming His Voice.

A Christ-centred Mission

Voice on the Rock Evangelical Mission exists to proclaim Jesus Christ, build lives through the truth of Scripture, and raise faithful voices for the work of God.

Our life together is shaped by worship, prayer, Bible teaching, evangelism, discipleship, fellowship and practical service.

Vision

To raise faithful voices who proclaim Christ and reflect His character.

Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, make disciples and equip believers for faithful service.

Foundation

Jesus Christ is the Rock upon whom the Mission stands.
